# Data Availability Statement

## Text for Editorial Manager

> The data underlying the results presented in this study are publicly
> available. Dataset A, EEG data for Mental Attention State Detection, is
> available from Kaggle:
> https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/inancigdem/eeg-data-for-mental-attention-state-detection.
> Dataset B, SAM 40, is available from Figshare:
> https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.14562090.v1. The author-generated
> analysis code, the configuration of every reported run, and the per-run
> artifacts underlying the reported figures and tables are available at
> https://github.com/sovetskiysn/eeg-validation-strategies under the MIT
> license, and archived on Zenodo under the DOI
> https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22255472.
