# Ethics statement for submission metadata

Use the following text, substantively unchanged, in the Ethics Statement field
of the submission system. It matches the ethics description in the clean
manuscript.

> This study is a secondary analysis of previously collected, publicly
> available, de-identified EEG data. No new data were collected and the
> authors had no contact with participants, therefore no additional ethical
> approval or participant consent was required for this study. The original
> data collection was approved as follows: for the mental attention-state
> dataset, the study was conducted in accordance with the ethical guidelines
> of Mersin University, with written informed consent obtained from all
> participants. For the SAM-40 dataset, the study was approved by the
> Institutional Ethics Committee of Gauhati University (GUIEC/2019/019,
> 15 October 2019), with informed consent obtained from all participants in
> accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki.

The original Dataset A publication does not report a formal ethics-committee
approval reference number. This statement therefore does not invent one.
